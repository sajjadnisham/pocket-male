Pocket Malé City

Malé & Hulhumalé in 3D · morning · experimental

A bright morning on real streets. The coastline, every road and the mapped buildings come from OpenStreetMap; the shops line the ground floors, scooters crowd the kerbs, people are out on the pavements and the blue buses run Boduthakurufaanu Magu, Majeedhee Magu and Hulhumalé’s main roads.

  • Nineteen situations are real ground-floor shops — find the tea shop, the pharmacy, the bank, and step inside to talk.
  • Run along the street, Fly over the island, or use the map to cross to Hulhumalé.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Blocks OpenStreetMap doesn’t map yet are filled with generic buildings; shopfronts, scooters, people and buses are generated to bring the streets to life. Dhivehi not yet reviewed by a native speaker.
