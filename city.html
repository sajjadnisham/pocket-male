Pocket Malé City

Real Malé in 3D · experimental

Every street, the coastline and the mapped buildings come from OpenStreetMap. Fourteen situations sit in their real places — the Fish Market, the Local Market, IGMH, the bus terminal, a ferry jetty, and a tea shop, bank, pharmacy and barber where real ones are. Walk up to a glowing door, go inside, and have the conversation.

  • Run for longer streets, Fly to see the whole island, Places or the map to jump across town.
  • Live Street View follows you, showing the nearest real 360° photosphere to where you are standing.
