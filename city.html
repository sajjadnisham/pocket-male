Pocket Malé City

Malé & Hulhumalé in 3D · morning · experimental

A bright morning on real streets. The coastline, every road and the mapped buildings come from OpenStreetMap; the businesses, masjids and offices sit where OpenStreetMap puts them, scooters crowd the kerbs, people are out walking and the blue buses run Boduthakurufaanu Magu, Majeedhee Magu and Hulhumalé’s main roads.

  • Over a thousand real places are situations you can walk into — tea shops, restaurants, fihaara, bakeries, offices, tailors, garages and 29 masjids. Twenty-one kinds in all, each with its own conversation and Dhivehi audio.
  • Bikes and a few cars weave through the traffic and stop for you. Interlocking-paver lanes inside, tarred ring roads outside, and the Sinamalé Bridge to Hulhumalé — walk it, or Fly.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Blocks OpenStreetMap doesn’t map yet are filled with generic buildings; shopfronts, scooters, people and buses are generated to bring the streets to life. Dhivehi not yet reviewed by a native speaker.
